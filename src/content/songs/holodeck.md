This went, more or less, according to plan. I had just kind of a bass line in my head, I sequenced it in Ableton, and then added a very simple drum loop on top of it. I was feeling like I wanted psychadelic rock of some kind, and I did instrumental versions that were okay, but I knew that just the simplest of lyrics on top would make a big difference. It ended up kind of "jam band" too.
