I wrote the chrous melody and all the lyrics for this one, eventually, but started with just the first chorus as a vocal sample. I actually re-recorded the vocal sample, because the timing was off in the off the cuff first one, and it was affecting about half of the products. Followed the same basic path I've kind of settled into now with Suno: get an idea in there, write a chorus and maybe one verse, see how it sounds with the styles I want, and adjust from there.

I spent way too much time listening to these over and over trying to find a good one. Honestly, many of them got disqualified immediately because Suno tried to make the second chorus "stomp-clap", which I wanted to save for the chorus after the bridge. Probably wound up with ear fatigue, and it's getting late, so here's 3 options. **YOU be the judge!**
