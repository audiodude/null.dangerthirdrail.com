Another new experience with Suno. I started trying to write a rock song in Ableton without any instruments. Eventually I had a pretty good 8 bar loop. I realized I could use 8 bars as the "intro" and then do an 8 bar verse. So far so good.

At that point, I realized that all the samples I had used to make the original 8 bars would have to be really meticulously chopped up and redone in order to add any kind of what we call *harmonic movement* (otherwise known as a "chord progression"). So I was going to give up there.

The turn came when I thought *"oh I should just feed this into Suno"*. Then I thought *"Well if I'm going to write lyrics, I might as well write a melody too, and how else am I going to get the lyrics and melody in there but to sing them?"*

So I fed into suno not anything like a complete song, but a promising 8 bars. I wrote the rest of the lyrics and picked the styles and here we are.
