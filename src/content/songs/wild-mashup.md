With this, I was trying just to go for the most wild mashup of styles/genres I could. I gave it the sample of glitch techno, told it to write lyrics as "an alt country ballad" and the style was "hard rock grunge".

The first attempt was very very "generic Suno". However, when I dialed up the audio influence tab, this is what I got.

Of course, the weakest link is the lyrics, and the fact that the heavy metal guitars sound pretty cool made me think *"hey actually I might be able to work with this!"*. To me, that's the ultimate fun of Suno.

And after sitting with it for like a half hour, I couldn't resist going back, writing a full set of my own lyrics, iterating etc as I always do. The result is "Burning Lava".
