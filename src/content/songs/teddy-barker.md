For this one, I wrote and recorded a guitar part, and I had a melody. I started coming up with lyrics and then I thought: If I'm going to put this into Suno anyway, why bother recording lyrics over and over? I'll just sing gibberish to guide the melody and iterate on the lyrics in Suno itself.

One kind of crazy part of this, I accidentally left *unbearable amounts of reverb* on the sample I uploaded to Suno, and didn't even realize it until later. It's a little production trick to put a ton of reverb on when a mediocre singer is performing, it gives them more confidence (this trick works on myself). Somehow Suno was able to **extract the exact melody anyways**.

For the purposes of this site, I've exported a new copy of the sample with the reverb off, so you can hear my fun gibberish.
